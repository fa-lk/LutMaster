Quick help

Images & navigation

  • Load photos: Drag one or more images into the workspace.
  • Compare: Hold the image to view the original; release to return to the grade.
  • Curve readout: Hover over the image to locate its input level on the active tone curve.
  • Zoom: Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom around the cursor; double-click the image to fit.
  • Shortcuts: \ hold = original, W split, J clipping, H histogram, [ ] previous / next photo, 0 fit.

Curves & adjustments

  • Reset: Double-click a slider or setting to restore its default.
  • Edit curves: Click to add a point; drag to adjust; double-click a point to remove it.

Loaded LUTs

  • Reorder: Drag a LUT using its ⋮ handle to change processing order.
